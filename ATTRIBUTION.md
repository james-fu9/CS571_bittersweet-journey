## Geographic data

- [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/): public-domain land, rivers, lakes and regions.
- [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright): © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL; includes Great Wall geometry.
- [WorldClim 2.1](https://www.worldclim.org/data/worldclim21.html): BIO12 annual precipitation, 1970–2000, at 10 arc-minute resolution. The 400 mm contour represents a long-term average.
- Copernicus DEM GLO-90: © DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014–2018, distributed by the European Union/ESA.

Source metadata is retained in the packaged datasets. Further details: [physical atlas](https://github.com/esstherc/bittersweet-journey/blob/5e54637a490e28b41151c587d6752add62f445ea/docs/atlas-physical.md) and [My Hometown](https://github.com/esstherc/bittersweet-journey/tree/5e54637a490e28b41151c587d6752add62f445ea/docs/chapters/my-hometown). Maps are literary reading aids, not navigation tools.
